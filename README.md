# WizWords

Greetings, weary adventurer. 

A harrowing challenge awaits you: a [trial](https://danielledonnelly.github.io/wizwords/) only for the swift of hand.

You must battle a series of powerful wizards using only your words. 

Harness your wit and agility to cast spells as quickly as possible. 

The longer the incantation, the more powerful it will be.

But beware! If you err, your spell will miss. Accuracy is even more crucial than speed.

And if I may, I suggest listening to the following [ballad](https://soundcloud.com/conepoem/evil-fucking-wizard) as you enter battle.
